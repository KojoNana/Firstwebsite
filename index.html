A flexible rhythm

Ideas for a healthier day

Use these as inspiration, not a strict schedule. Adapt them to your needs and routine.

WHEN YOU WAKE UP
🌅

Ease into your morning

  • Open the curtains or step outside for some daylight.
  • Have a drink of water if you feel thirsty.
  • Take a few minutes to stretch or decide what matters today.
DURING THE DAY
🌱

Take small reset breaks

  • Stand up, change position, or move gently when you can.
  • Build meals around foods you enjoy, including produce when available.
  • Pause between tasks to relax your shoulders and take a few slow breaths.
BEFORE BED
🌙

Help yourself wind down

  • Choose a calming activity, like reading, music, or a warm shower.
  • Make your sleeping space comfortable and quiet if possible.
  • Give yourself permission to rest; tomorrow is another opportunity.

The bigger picture

Wellness is more than a checklist

There isn't one perfect routine. Focus on steady, kind choices and adjust them to what works for your body, culture, budget, and circumstances.

🍎

Eat in a way that works for you

Variety can be helpful, but meals don't need to be perfect. Choose nourishing foods that are accessible and enjoyable to you.

🤝

Stay connected

Make time for a check-in with someone you trust. Asking for support is a healthy step, too.

🩺

Look after your health

Keep up with routine care and ask a qualified health professional questions about symptoms, medicines, or changes that concern you.
